📋 Admission Process

Welcome to Wonderhills College

We are delighted that you are considering Wonderhills College for your child's education. Our admission process is designed to be simple, transparent, and welcoming.

📌 Admission Requirements

  • Age Declaration
  • Previous School Report Card (for transfer students)
  • Entrance Examination (for new students)

📅 Admission Steps

  • Step 1: Visit the school.
  • Step 2: Collect and complete the application form.
  • Step 3: Submit the completed form with required documents.
  • Step 4: Take the entrance examination.
  • Step 5: Receive admission decision via email or phone.
  • Step 6: Complete registration and fee payment.
